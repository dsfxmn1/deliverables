The honest read

At nearly 5, she's already at the pedal transition, so the "grows with them from age 2" pitch is partly spent — George would be buying a bike that's near the top of its (2–6) range and tops out as a 14″ pedal bike. Realistically 1–2 seasons before she outgrows it. That math flips if there's a younger sibling to hand it down to — then it's excellent value. If the goal is simply her first pedal bike, a lighter 14–16″ dedicated bike may serve longer.

Alternatives — if she's near the top of the range

At nearly 5 she's likely ready to ride a 16″ pedal bike directly — she may not need the balance→pedal journey at all. A 16″ will also last her noticeably longer than the LittleBig's 14″ pedal stage. These are the strongest 16″ first-pedal options, priced from the makers' own pages:
