What actually matters when choosing

  • Weight: lighter = safer to handle and quicker to learn. Aim under ~17 lb.
  • Fit, by inside leg — not age: she's ~39–46″ tall → 16″. Measure before buying.
  • Brakes: hand brakes (front + rear) beat a coaster-only setup for learning control.
  • Resale / hand-down: a 16″ she rides 2–3 seasons then passes on; the LittleBig only pays off if there's a sibling behind her.

Before you buy — checklist for George

  • Measure her height and inside leg.
  • Pick the size she'll grow into, not out of — for a near-5 that's 16″.
  • If the convertible route: confirm the LittleBig model year + matching pedal kit ("for Original (Pre-2025)").
  • Budget: ~$249 (Guardian) → ~$509 (Prevelo) for straight pedal; ~$449 + duty for the LittleBig.
