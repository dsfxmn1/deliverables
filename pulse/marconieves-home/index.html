The Series

Fractured Realities

A multiverse science-fiction series about the people the multiverse keeps tearing apart. Across a set of parallel New Yorks, ordinary lives collide between dimensions.

Book One

As The Sun Rises

Roman Martin wakes up with one sneaker and a memory his best friend says never happened. Nadia Rai remembers the same night, from a New York that isn’t his. They are crossing between realities without knowing how, and every crossing widens a crack that threatens every world at once.

About

Marco Nieves

MN

Marco Nieves is a Nuyorican writer born and raised in Manhattan’s East Village, in a Puerto Rican household where the culture was everyday life. Growing up, he was often too ethnic for some rooms and too gringo for others, and he learned early what it means to be caught between who you are and who the world will let you be. That tension lives in his work.

His debut novel, As The Sun Rises, opens the Fractured Realities series. He has also had two plays produced off-off-Broadway in New York.
